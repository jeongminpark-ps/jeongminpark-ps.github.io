Welcome! 

I am a Postdoctoral Prize Research Fellow in Politics at Nuffield College, Oxford University. My research examines how states adjudicate competing redistributive pressures created by armed conflict, focusing on state development and gender. Methodologically, I combine archival research with causal inference. You can see the latest version of my CV [here](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/utmlpihe9m8lm1kj5p7hb/Jeongmin_s_CV.pdf?rlkey=7mmt172gdq1dvvpgl6hd27392&st=mqcskf63&dl=0).


I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Politics at Princeton University in June 2025. My works are published or accepted in the *American Journal of Political Science* and *International Studies Quarterly*. You can reach me at jeongmin.park@nuffield.ox.ac.uk.
