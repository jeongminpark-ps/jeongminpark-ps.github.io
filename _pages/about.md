Welcome! 

I am a Postdoctoral Prize Research Fellow in Politics at Nuffield College, Oxford University. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Politics at Princeton University. I study the distributional consequences of international conflict, with a substantive focus on historical political economy, gender, and labor. You can see the latest version of my CV [here](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/9s4psiqnzqsm4w3mt45q9/Jeongmin_s_CV.pdf?rlkey=h1olbw76nhxbiy65c4rndblj2&st=2bq7gq05&dl=0).


My work has been published at the *International Studies Quarterly*. You can reach me at jp49@princeton.edu.
